# 0.2.12 pre-beta release notes · draft

SIGNAL//BREAK is a single-player roguelite deckbuilding RPG. Lead three heroes through a failing signal network, stitch attacks together in real time, and pass the chain between fighters. Campaign paths, rewards and bosses vary between runs. Discover EX sequences and unlock alternative starting relics through the Museum.

- The Core’s late attack sequence leaves more space between its strongest blows. Each point of Guard helps against Predator Needle. Vector Winch’s boost is shorter, and a latched Reserve card becomes the next draw.
- Tower Plate and Quick Exit preserve their roles with smaller Guard and payout bonuses. The Mirror Array now previews the actual variable damage of Reflection.
- Interrupted offline updates preserve the previous playable cache. Saving detects when another tab updated the active checkpoint. Unreadable records are protected from automatic overwrite.
- A paused battlefield redraws when needed. Scene and battle clocks reject backwards frame deltas. English and Russian descriptions and the Switchyard Journal hint reflect the current mechanics.

The web package and Swift Playgrounds project are versioned together. See the [beta packet](PLAYTEST.md), [technical report](PHASE12.md) and the device verification status there.

## Краткое описание

SIGNAL//BREAK — одиночная ролевая игра с колодой, случайными маршрутами и боями в реальном времени. Управляйте тремя героями, передавайте комбо Эстафетой и выбирайте свой путь к Ядру. Находите EX-комбо и открывайте в Музее новые стартовые реликвии.

В сборке 0.2.12 уточнены атаки Ядра, Иглы-хищника и Зеркального массива, ослаблена буксировка лебёдки и скорректированы бонусы двух предметов. Улучшена защита сохранений и офлайн-обновлений. [Памятка для бета-теста](PLAYTEST.md)
